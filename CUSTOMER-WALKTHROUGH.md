# Using the samples with a client

Start with the customer's outcome, audience and current process. Show only the sample that fits the conversation. These are fictional examples, not completed client commissions or promised production features.

| Customer need | Show | Let them try | Decisions for their project |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Be found and receive inquiries | Cedar & Fern website | Mobile navigation, services, editing and inquiry preview | Brand, copy, pages, domain, publishing access, inquiry destination, booking/payments |
| Work away from a desk | Field Notes for iPhone | Visit details, checklist, notes, offline changes and export | Target platforms, offline requirements, notifications, sync, user roles, store ownership |
| Organize recurring office work | Studio Board | New job, status change, search, relaunch and export | Operating systems, team access, imports, integrations, backup and installer delivery |

## A clear first conversation

1. Ask what success looks like, who uses it, what already exists, the budget range, deadline and required platforms.
2. Show the relevant sample and explain which parts work locally and which integrations need commissioning.
3. Record what the customer wants to keep, change or remove. Separate launch essentials from later ideas.
4. Return a written scope with deliverables, exclusions, acceptance checks, milestone payments, review windows and change handling. A sample is a starting point, not a quote.
5. Agree vendor ownership and access. The default is client-owned domain, hosting, store and service accounts with delegated HensTek access. Decide how ongoing care and handoff will work.
6. After the agreed contract and first payment, invite the customer to their private project portal. Keep milestones, decisions, deliverables and approved changes there; use email for formal approvals as agreed.

## Options to present

- **Starting point:** adapt a sample, build a custom experience, or begin with paid discovery when scope is uncertain.
- **Content:** a simple managed website, customer editing with authenticated publishing, or periodic HensTek updates under an agreed service.
- **Accounts:** client-owned accounts managed by HensTek by default. A managed hosting package or HensTek-owned subscription product creates different ownership, support and recurring billing obligations and requires its own agreement.
- **Delivery:** a website, iPhone app, desktop tool, or a separately scoped combination. These samples do not imply cross-device sync or coverage of every platform.
- **After launch:** customer handoff, separately agreed care, or a later enhancement phase. Do not imply automatic monthly charges or continuous availability.

Before a demonstration, restore fictional data and keep real customer documents, credentials and records out of the public sample. A customer's commissioned project belongs in its own appropriately private repository and accounts.
